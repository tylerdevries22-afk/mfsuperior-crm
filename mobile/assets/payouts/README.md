# Payout brand assets

These bundled marks are used only to identify supported payout rails in the
MF Superior app. They remain unmodified except for rasterization and sizing.

- Venmo: official Venmo favicon from the [Venmo brand page](https://venmo.com/about/brand).
- Cash App: official Cash App mark from the [Cash App developer assets](https://developers.cash.app/cash-app-pay-partner-api/guides/resources/cash-app-pay-assets).
- Zelle: official logo from [Zelle](https://www.zelle.com/sites/default/files/2026-02/Zelle-logo-no-tagline-RGB-purple.png).
- Apple Cash: cropped Apple Cash wordmark from [Apple's Apple Cash page](https://www.apple.com/apple-cash/).

Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, and Apple Cash are trademarks of their respective
owners. MF Superior is not affiliated with or endorsed by those services.
